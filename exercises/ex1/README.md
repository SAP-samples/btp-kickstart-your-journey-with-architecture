## Introduction to the Architecture Center <br>
To follow up on the given requirements you decide to have a look if there are some guidelines to support the given case.

1. Navigate back to the SAP BTP Guidance Framework<br>
![Guidance Framework2](./images/7.png) <br>

You decide to have a look if a reference architecture is available that fits your requirements

2. Navigate to the SAP Architecture Center <br>

![Architecture Center](./images/8.png) <br>

3. To get an overview about the content in the Architecture Center you press the button to "Explore Now" to get to the Architecture Explorer<br>
4. browse through the different scenarios to get an understanding about the possibilities and the content that is provided in the SAP Architecture Center<br>

As you are looking for a reference architecture to support the extension of Joule's capabilities for your end users you should look for the possibility to filter the available reference architectures down to artificial intelligent related ones.<br>

5. filter the architectures by technology domain "artificial intelligence"

![Architecture Center](./images/9.png) <br>

6. Have a look at the recommended items. Choose the scenario that fots your case ("Extending Joule with Joule Studio")<br>

![Architecture Explorer2](./images/10.png)<br>

7. Get familiar with the structure how the SAP Architecture Center describes the reference architectures in detail. Have a look at the list of services and components and use the solution diagramm and the flow description to understand the architecture that is described.

![Architecture Explorer2](./images/11.png)<br>
